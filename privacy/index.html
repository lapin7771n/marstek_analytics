Battery ETASupport

Privacy policy

Effective 15 October 2026

Battery ETA does not collect, share or sell any personal data. It has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What the app stores on your device

  • The battery IP address, port and reserve you enter.
  • Recent readings from your battery, used to draw the app and its widgets.

This data stays in the app's storage on your iPhone or iPad. It never leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Network use

The app sends status requests only to the battery address on your local Wi‑Fi. It contacts no other server.

Third parties

None. If you turned on sharing with app developers in iOS settings, Apple may send crash reports to the developer. Apple's privacy policy covers that.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

A changed policy gets a new effective date on this page.
