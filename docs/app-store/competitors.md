# Battery ETA — App Store competitors

Checked 2026-10-16 through the iTunes Search API (storefronts UA, DE, PL, US) and the apps' store pages.

## Direct: apps that read a Marstek battery

| App | Maker | Rating | Price | What it does | Gap Battery ETA fills |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| MARSTEK (official) | Hamedata | 4.6 (799) UA · 3.6 (2,055) DE | Free | Cloud account, Bluetooth/Wi‑Fi setup, modes, schedules | No time-left figure, no iOS widget, login prompts, cloud drop-outs |
| APEXpulse Battery Control | APEXone bv (NL) | 1 rating | Free, Pro $14.99 | Modbus TCP: 16-cell view, history, writes charge settings. EN/NL/DE | No Ukrainian, no outage-schedule link, writes to the battery |

Users of the official app ask for exactly what Battery ETA does:

- UA, 3★: "Не відображається час до повного розрядження та час до повного заряду."
- NL, 3★: "Please add widgets to the app … on the Home Screen."
- UA, 3★: asks for a push notice when the battery stops giving backup power.

APEXpulse ships the subtitle "Real-time control for Marstek", so App Review accepts the brand as a compatibility statement.

## Position

Battery ETA is the only app that joins a live Marstek reading with the YASNO outage schedule: "Lasts until 18:00, 1 h 20 min to spare". It is free, has no account, no ads and no in-app purchases, and only reads the battery.
